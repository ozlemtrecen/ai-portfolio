About me

Clinical microbiology meets AI-assisted research practice.

Professional profile

I am a medical doctor and medical microbiology specialist working across diagnostic microbiology, culture-based methods, antimicrobial susceptibility testing, public health laboratory workflows, and quality-oriented laboratory practice.

My research interests include vaccines, respiratory virus epidemiology, surveillance, machine learning in diagnostic microbiology, and science communication. I am especially interested in how generative AI can support clearer writing, faster evidence organization, more reproducible analysis, and better communication without compromising scientific integrity.

About the course

Generative AI for Work & Research

8-week learning portfolio

The course explores practical generative AI skills for professional and research settings, including content creation, document workflows, presentations, no-code websites, automated data analysis, literature review support, knowledge management, custom chatbots, task automation and a final capstone project.

01

Writing and documents

Using AI to structure reports, manuscripts, summaries and professional communication while preserving accuracy and authorship.

02

Presentations and websites

Designing clear academic outputs, visual explanations and web-based portfolios without frameworks.

03

Data analysis support

Exploring AI-assisted cleaning, analysis planning, visualization and reproducibility checks.

04

Literature workflows

Supporting search strategy, evidence mapping, synthesis and structured reading notes.

05

Custom assistants

Designing domain-specific chatbots for research, education, project management and communication.

06

Responsible practice

Keeping outputs transparent, privacy-conscious, verifiable and ready for expert review.
